## About

I turn raw footage into engaging visual stories — clean edits, smooth transitions and strong pacing that keep viewers hooked. From reels and social clips to long-form YouTube and promos, every edit blends creativity with purpose. Every frame counts.

**Why work with me:** Retention-first pacing · Clean, smooth transitions · Edits tailored to platform & audience · Fast, clear communication

1. **Instagram Reels** — Scroll-proof edits with a hook in the first second, clean captions and rhythm that fits the trend. *Ideal for: creators & small brands.*
